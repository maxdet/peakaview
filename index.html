Peak A View

Slow TV from the Swiss Alps. Every good morning, a fixed camera on a terrace near Lucerne records the sunrise over Rigi, the Mythen and the Rossberg, and the light on the peaks from Titlis to Pilatus. The result is a quiet daily timelapse with gentle music and natural ambience.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@peakaview
